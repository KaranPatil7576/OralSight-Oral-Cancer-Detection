ABOUT THIS MODEL

How the classification works

1. Image upload: Choose a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image up to 10 MiB. The app checks the file type and size and shows a preview; these checks do not assess the image.

2. Preprocessing: The image is sent to the prediction service at /api/v1/predictions. Image resizing, normalization, and other preprocessing happen in that service. Their exact steps are not included in this project.

3. CNN classification: The service runs its image model and returns one of two dataset labels: Cancer or Non-Cancer. The model architecture and exact decision rule are not included here, so they cannot be specified from this project’s code.

4. Result: The app displays the returned class, score, and model version. The score is a model score, not a probability of cancer. If supplied, Grad-CAM visualizes image regions that influenced the model; it is not a clinical explanation.

Limitations: This is an educational research prototype, not a diagnostic tool. It has not been validated for clinical use; do not use it to diagnose, screen, or guide treatment. Consult a qualified dental or medical professional about concerns.
